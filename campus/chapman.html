How it works

Three steps to your first night out.

01Sign up with your .edu

Your Chapman email verifies you as a student. We email a confirmation code to finish sign-up.

02Open the feed or the map

Scroll what’s on tonight and this weekend, or open Campus Pulse to see where the crowd is right now.

03RSVP or grab a ticket

See which friends are going, then RSVP or check out on Stripe. Your QR ticket lives in Wallet and scans at the door.

Questions

Chapman questions, answered.

Is Frapp free for Chapman students?

Yes. Frapp is free to download and free to use for students. When an event sells tickets, the fee is shown in the all-in price before you pay.

Who can join Frapp at Chapman?

Chapman students sign up with their .edu email. Every account is a verified student, so everyone on the feed actually goes here.

Can my club or org post events?

Yes. Hosts publish events in minutes, watch RSVPs come in live, scan tickets at the door from their phone, and get paid out through Stripe.

What if I can’t make it to an event I bought a ticket for?

You can request a refund up to 24 hours before the event starts, as long as your ticket hasn’t been scanned at the door. The deadline is the same for every event on Frapp.

Is Frapp run by Chapman University?

No. Frapp is built by Chapman students and run by Frapp LLC in Orange, CA. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Chapman University.
